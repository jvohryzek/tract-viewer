Atlas source & credit

600 display tracks from the HCP-842 atlas (Yeh et al., 2018), accessed through the LQT toolbox (Griffis et al., 2021). Original tract folder · Sampling & coordinate conversion. Highlighted tracks are intersections in this display subsample, not a full-atlas disconnection estimate.

Atlas-derived data: CC BY-SA 4.0 and WU-Minn HCP data-use terms. Viewer code: MIT.
